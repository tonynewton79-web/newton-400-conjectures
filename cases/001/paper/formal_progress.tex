\section{Formal certification: progressive proof record}\label{sec:formal}
A reproducible Lean~4/mathlib~v4.24.0 implementation now certifies
several of the exact algebraic and combinatorial identities needed for
Lemma~\ref{lem:gram}. The formal sources and the resolved dependency
manifest are retained at
\href{https://github.com/tonynewton79-web/newton-400-conjectures}{the
public Newton Astro Labs research repository}. We distinguish each
kernel-checked lemma from the \emph{not yet kernel-certified} complete
spectral graph theorem.

\paragraph{Stage 1: positive Gram mechanism.}
The module \texttt{NewtonConjectures.lean} certifies the two strict
rational comparison margins $506/11025$ and $1733/58800$, the
reciprocal second-difference identity, the positivity of the interior
weights, and nonnegativity of abstract weighted Gram quadratic forms.
These are algebraic and rational statements; they do not alone
certify the cyclic spectral conclusion.

\paragraph{Stage 2: universal reciprocal-distance coefficients.}
The module \texttt{CycleKernel.lean} proves the finite telescoping identity
for every function $f:\mathbb N\to\mathbb Q$ and specializes it to
$f(j)=1/j$. For $1\le d<m$ its exact certificate is
\begin{align}\label{eq:lean-kernel}
\frac1d={}&\frac1m+
\sum_{j=d+1}^{m-1}
(j-d)\frac{2}{(j-1)j(j+1)}\notag\\
&+\frac{m-d}{m(m-1)}.
\end{align}

\paragraph{Stage 3: genuine cyclic matrix products.}
The modules \texttt{CyclicIntersection.lean},
\texttt{CyclicZMod.lean}, and \texttt{CyclicGram.lean}
define the cyclic incidence matrix over $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$,
using $B_j(u,k)=1$ exactly when the residue $k-u$ lies in
$\{0,\ldots,j-1\}$. For \emph{all} vertices $u,v$
and $2j\le n$, the Lean kernel proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lean-overlap}
(B_jB_j^{\mathsf T})_{uv}
=\max\{j-d(u,v),0\}.
\end{equation}
The formal proof handles cyclic wraparound, disjoint intervals,
and even-order antipodes, and evaluates an actual \texttt{Matrix.mul},
rather than merely asserting a counting analogy.

\paragraph{Stage 4: diagonal normalization.}
The module \texttt{GramDiagonal.lean} proves for every $m\ge2$ that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lean-diag}
\frac1m+\sum_{j=2}^{m-1}\frac{2}{(j-1)(j+1)}
+\frac1{m-1}=\frac32.
\end{equation}
It additionally certifies the corresponding diagonal \emph{of the
actual weighted incidence Gram matrices} for every $n\ge4$
and every vertex, with $m=\lfloor n/2\rfloor$.

\paragraph{Stage 5: universal off-diagonal sum.}
The new module \texttt{OffDiagonal.lean} proves for every $m\ge2$
and every $1\le d\le m$ that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lean-offdiag}
\frac1m+\sum_{j=2}^{m}w_j(j-d)_+=\frac1d,
\end{equation}
where $(x)_+=\max(x,0)$ and the $w_j$ are precisely those of
\eqref{eq:weights}. Importantly, the formal statement uses natural
truncated subtraction, so the $d=m$ endpoint is included and
no sign convention is silently assumed.

\paragraph{Independent replay and kernel gate.}
The Stage~5 GitHub Actions run
\href{https://github.com/tonynewton79-web/newton-400-conjectures/actions/runs/39131413230}{39131413230}
completed all 7,358 Lean build jobs. An independent axiom audit
accepted 100 declarations using only the foundational allowlist
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound};
the proof-placeholder check passed. In a separate exact
\texttt{fractions.Fraction} replay, all 7,259 pairs $(m,d)$
with $2\le m\le120$ and $1\le d\le m$ passed
\eqref{eq:lean-offdiag}. No floating-point acceptance criterion
was used for these claims. The complete
\href{https://github.com/tonynewton79-web/newton-400-conjectures/blob/main/cases/001/LEAN_STATUS.md}{Lean proof ledger}
separates verified and outstanding obligations.

\paragraph{Precise boundary of formal certification.}
Equations \eqref{eq:lean-overlap}, \eqref{eq:lean-diag},
and \eqref{eq:lean-offdiag}, taken together, supply
entrywise components of \eqref{eq:gram}. However, the single
all-entry matrix theorem and the subsequent cycle-specific
spectral bound must still be assembled and checked \emph{inside
Lean}. In particular, the ordering of the cycle adjacency
eigenvalues, the two uniform harmonic comparisons, and
the exact finite converse $C_3,\ldots,C_{14}$ have not
all been promoted to a complete Lean proof.
The analytical theorem and the finite exact certificates are
presented independently of this partial formalization.
