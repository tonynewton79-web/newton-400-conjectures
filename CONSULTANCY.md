# Newton Astro Labs — research consultancy

**Scientific research · mathematical verification · computational discovery**

Newton Astro Labs offers scoped, independent technical investigations for mathematicians, physicists, computer scientists, engineers, and research-led teams. Enquiries and agreed deliverables are handled separately from the open research cases maintained in this repository.

## Research services

| Service | Price per project | Included research time |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Research & Verification | **£100** | Up to 4 hours |
| Advanced Research Investigation | **£500** | Up to 8 hours |

### Research & Verification — £100

A focused investigation of a mathematical claim, draft, derivation, algorithm or modelling question. Depending on the work agreed, this may include targeted proof checks, symbolic or numerical calculations, counterexample searches, and a concise written report.

### Advanced Research Investigation — £500

A deeper investigation of a conjecture, proposed theorem, algorithm, or theoretical direction. Depending on the project, deliverables may include:

- a structured deep-dive into the underlying mathematics or physics;
- **purpose-built Python scripts, tests, numerical experiments or simulations**, where useful;
- counterexample searches, limiting-case checks and reproducibility analysis;
- an extended technical report with methods, evidence, limitations, next steps and relevant code/results.

The advanced package covers **up to eight hours** of agreed research time. It does not guarantee a new theorem, successful formal proof, or particular finding.

## Delivery and payment

**Delivery target:** Within 48 hours after the project scope is accepted and the required materials are received, subject to capacity and feasibility. **Payment:** PayPal invoice after the agreed report/materials are delivered, under agreed terms. Clients should not send confidential or unpublished material before any necessary confidentiality and tool-use permissions are in place.
